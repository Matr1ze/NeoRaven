Material Components for Android — Minecraft 1.8.9 adaptation

This work includes adaptations of algorithms from Material Components for Android:
https://github.com/material-components/material-components-android

Adapted files/algorithms:
- CircularDrawingDelegate.java: circular path, inward wave anchors, cubic handles,
  round caps, gaps and near-completion seam handling.
- CircularIndeterminateRetreatAnimatorDelegate.java: retreat timing and segment/rotation values.
- DeterminateDrawable.java: spring parameters and amplitude transition behavior.
- Progress indicator and motion token resources: default dimensions, colors and easing.

Modifications: removed Android APIs, used Java2D path measurement/rasterization,
added LWJGL 2 texture rendering, Minecraft GUI integration and headless verification.
This is an independent adaptation and is not an official Google or Mojang component.
